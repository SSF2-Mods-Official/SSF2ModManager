---
title: Controller rumble
summary: Vibration on menus, hits, and the title screen.
order: 110
---

Controller rumble is on in SSF2 Shell for controllers that support it. You do not turn it on from a separate menu. If the controller can rumble, the game uses it.

What it responds to:

- A short pulse on menu moves
- The title screen, for the controller that is active
- Hits in a match, scaled by the knockback
- A stop when a menu closes or a player should stop vibrating

If nothing happens, the controller or the driver is not reporting rumble. The mod is already loaded. There is no second switch in the manager.
