Palette Maker is the in-game recolor studio. The original Palette Maker was made by VAL. SSF2 Shell keeps that studio and opens it from the manager.

## How to open it

- **Make Mods**, then **Costume**
- **Configure**, starting menu **Vault**, action **Palette Maker**, then pick the character
- **Manage Mods**, **Costume gallery**, to look at costumes you already saved

## Recoloring

Pick the character and the costume slot, edit the colors, and save. The costume is stored with your other user data in SSF2 Shell. The next time you are on the character select screen, that costume is one of the slots for that fighter.

You do not have to clone the character to recolor them. A clone is a separate fighter. A palette is a color set on a fighter you already have.

## While the palette UI is hidden

A bar sits at the bottom of the studio:

- **Show UI** brings the color tools back.
- **Change music** opens My Jukebox for this studio.
- **Change stage** opens the stage select screen and reloads the studio on the stage you pick.
- **Change time of day** is only on the rest area.
- **Go to rest area** reloads the studio on the rest area stage.

The stage you used last, and the song, are remembered per character. Changing the stage clears that character's saved song so the new stage plays its own music until you pick another song.
