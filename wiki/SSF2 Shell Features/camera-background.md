Camera background lets the match use another stage's backdrop: the moving layers, not just a flat picture. The stage you are fighting on stays the one you picked. The song stays whatever [My Jukebox](my-jukebox.md) is playing. The background does not change the music.

## How to set it

On the stage select screen, **Shield** on a stage marks that stage's camera background for the match. The next stage you actually start uses that backdrop.

From the [Mod menu](mod-menu.md), the stage select entry can wait for an Attack press. That press confirms the camera background only, so you do not have to start the match to lock it in.

**Space BG** uses the menu space art for this session instead of a stage.

## Online

The host's camera background is the one the guest sees. Each player can still have the stage's own song.
