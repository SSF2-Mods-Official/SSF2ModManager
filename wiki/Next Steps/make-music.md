Music for stages and menus lives in SSF2 Shell. Finish [Set up Mod Manager](../Start Here/setup.md) first so Next2Flash and ffmpeg are ready.

## 1 — Jukebox (in game)

**Make Mods → Music** opens the Shell jukebox (My Jukebox). Add tracks, build playlists, and test in menus or matches from there.

## 2 — Trim / prepare an MP3

When you import a track that needs Flash-friendly audio, the manager uses the **bundled** `runtime\ffmpeg` only (no system install). Trim and resample in the music tools when the UI offers it.

## 3 — Package and share

**Package mods** can include music packs. On GameBanana, music mods use the Music category when that feed has entries.

See also the Dev Wiki page Music player for how the built-in mod hooks playback.
