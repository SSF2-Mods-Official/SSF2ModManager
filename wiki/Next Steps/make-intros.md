Boot intros are short videos the shell can play at startup. Finish [Set up Mod Manager](../Start Here/setup.md) first.

## 1 — Add an intro

On the launcher, click **Make Mods**, then **Intros**.

## 2 — Convert

Pick your video file. The manager converts it with the **bundled** ffmpeg under `runtime\ffmpeg` (nothing on PATH required).

## 3 — Enable and test

Turn the intro on in Manage Mods or Configure Launch if the UI offers a toggle, then **Play** and confirm it runs at boot.

GameBanana does not have a dedicated Intros Mod category yet; share the file manually or inside a general mod pack.
