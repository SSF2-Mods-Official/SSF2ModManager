An event is a match copied from a stock or expansion event. Finish [Set up Mod Manager](../Start Here/setup.md) first.

## 1 — Open Make Mods

On the launcher, click **Make Mods**, then **Events**, then **Make a new event**.

## 2 — Fill the form

**Clone from** lists the stock events, then expansion events that have a script. The name box stays empty. The description box fills with that event's original text. Name and description are both required.

The rest of the form is the preview picture, stage, stage background, music, stocks, minutes, hazards, team damage, and each fighter's character, costume, human, level, and stocks. **Player chooses a character** is the last checkbox. Type, record, how you win, and items stay in the script.

Create copies the event you picked and changes only those fields.

## 3 — Create

**Create** writes the pack and compiles it. **Create and test** does that and launches the game into that event.

**Compile an event** is a separate screen. Pick a pack that already exists, then **Compile** or **Compile and test**.

## 4 — Play

**Play** or **Debug**, then open Event Mode and pick your event. The new pack is already on. **Create and test** and **Compile and test** open that event for you.

GameBanana lists events under the Events category when you upload a pack from **Package**.
