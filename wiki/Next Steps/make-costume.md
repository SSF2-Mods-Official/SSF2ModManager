Costumes are recolors, not a separate clone/compile pipeline. Finish [Set up Mod Manager](../Start Here/setup.md) first.

## 1 — Open the palette maker

On the launcher, click **Make Mods**, then **Costume** (or launch the palette maker from the tools list). That opens the in-game recolor tool in SSF2 Shell.

## 2 — Recolor and save

Pick a character and costume slot, edit the palette, and save. The shell stores the costume with the other user data under `expansions/user`.

## 3 — Use it in a match

**Play**, open Character Select, pick that fighter and the costume you saved.

There is no separate Package / Download Mods category for palette costumes yet — share them as part of a character pack or vault export when you use those flows.
